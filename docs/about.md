# About This Site

<div class="itsd-page-intro" markdown>

## The working memory of IT Service Delivery

This site is the shared working library for IT Service Delivery. It turns repeated questions, proven fixes, and important handoffs into guidance people can use during the next ticket, change, or escalation.

</div>

## What you can do here

- **Find documents:** Search the knowledge base or open the Documents tab to filter the shared library by name, type, and category.
- **Upload material:** Add an approved Markdown document, PDF, or supporting file through the Upload Documents page.
- **Follow a handoff:** Use escalation and de-escalation guidance to understand ownership and the next action.
- **Suggest an improvement:** Open Support to report a gap, propose clearer guidance, or request a useful feature.
- **Review changes:** Open the Change Log to see the technical history of the site and its documentation.

## What belongs here

- Procedures and decision points that are approved for team use.
- Service context, ownership, prerequisites, and escalation paths.
- Troubleshooting notes that help analysts resolve recurring issues.
- Training material, definitions, and useful operational examples.

Do not publish passwords, tokens, private keys, personal data, confidential customer information, or unapproved production details.

## How to keep it useful

1. Write for the person handling the next ticket, not for the person who already knows the answer.
2. State the scope, prerequisites, owner, and stop or escalation conditions.
3. Use searchable terms that match the language people use in tickets.
4. Test procedures in the appropriate environment before calling them ready.
5. Update or retire guidance when the underlying process changes.

## Site ownership

The site is a static site with its source maintained in a version-controlled repository. Sign-in and repository permissions may be required to upload, edit, or delete files. Changes should be reviewed and committed through the normal review process.

When in doubt, go to **Support** and explain what would have made the last ticket easier.
