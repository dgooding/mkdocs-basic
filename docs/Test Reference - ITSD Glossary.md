# Test Reference - ITSD Glossary

This reference glossary defines common IT Service Delivery terms in plain language. Team-specific names, targets, and approval rules take precedence where they are documented in an approved policy.

## Terms

**Change**
: An addition, modification, or removal that could affect an IT service.

**Configuration item (CI)**
: A component that must be managed to deliver a service, such as an application, server, network device, or documented relationship.

**Escalation**
: A deliberate transfer of work, authority, or attention to a team or person with the required expertise, access, or decision-making authority.

**Incident**
: An unplanned interruption to a service or a reduction in the quality of a service.

**Knowledge article**
: A maintained piece of guidance that helps someone diagnose, resolve, or understand a recurring task or issue.

**Major incident**
: An incident with significant business impact that requires coordinated response and enhanced communication. The local major-incident policy defines the threshold.

**Priority**
: The order in which work should be handled, normally based on impact and urgency. Priority is not a measure of how important a requester feels their issue is.

**Problem**
: The underlying cause, or a suspected cause, of one or more incidents.

**Resolver group**
: The team accountable for investigating or completing a particular class of work.

**Request**
: A user's request for information, access, a standard service, or an approved change.

**Service level agreement (SLA)**
: A documented commitment about service performance or support, including targets and responsibilities.

**Service owner**
: The person accountable for the service's outcomes, lifecycle, risks, and stakeholder decisions.

**Service request**
: A request for a predefined, repeatable service action, such as approved access or standard equipment.

**Workaround**
: A temporary way to reduce or avoid the effect of an incident while a permanent fix is developed.

## How to use this glossary

- Prefer these terms in ticket titles, handoffs, and knowledge articles so search results remain consistent.
- Do not use “incident,” “problem,” and “request” interchangeably; the distinction affects workflow and ownership.
- Link to the authoritative policy when a definition includes a local threshold, target, or approval requirement.
- Review the glossary when a team introduces a new service, resolver group, or operating model.
